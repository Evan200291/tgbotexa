# Exabyte Digital Store — Design System & Cyber Blues Liquid UI (`docs/DESIGN.md`)

## 1. Visual Concept
**"Cyber Blues & Neon Tech" + Liquid Glass UI**
Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`) and Cyber Blue (`#38BDF8` / `#0284C7`) on deep cyber obsidian slate (`#070B14`, `#10192E`), with cyber emerald (`#10B981`) and cyber crimson (`#F43F5E`) accents. Renders on frosted cyber glass cards (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(160%)`) with specular top-edge highlights, cyan neon corner HUD brackets, cyber diamond dividers (`◈`), and a high-tech perspective grid horizon.

## 3. Burmese Typography Rules
- **Headings:** `"Noto Serif Myanmar"` (`600/700`), fallback `"Noto Sans Myanmar"`, `system-ui`.
- **Body & UI:** `"Noto Sans Myanmar"` (`400/500/700`), minimum `16px`, `line-height: 1.75`.
- **No clipping:** Never apply `letter-spacing`, `text-transform`, `overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis` on single-line Burmese labels, or fixed heights that clip stacked upper/lower Myanmar diacritics. All interactive buttons have `min-height: 48px` and wrap cleanly.
- **Digits:** Western digits (`0-9`) for prices, GB, and dates (`5,000 ကျပ်`, `30 ရက်`).
